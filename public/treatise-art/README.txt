Artwork for /treatise/ (the illuminated-manuscript treatment).

Every file here is original, drawn as SVG paths and filters by
tools/treatise-art.py (python3 tools/treatise-art.py public/treatise-art).
Nothing is traced or copied from any manuscript, scan or stock source; the
bar-and-ivy border, diaper, penwork, sun and star are drawn in a period
style only.

  vine-v.svg / vine-h.svg       bar border with ivy (vertical, horizontal tiles)
  boss.svg                      corner boss
  diaper.svg                    lozenge diaper ground for miniatures
  penwork.svg                   white penwork over painted grounds
  vellum.svg / leather.svg      turbulence textures for the leaf and lectern
  marg-sun.svg / marg-star.svg  marginal drolleries
