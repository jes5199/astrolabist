Next the forseide Cercle of the Abc., under the cros-lyne, is marked the scale, in maner of two squyres, or elles in manere of laddres, that serveth by hise 12 poyntes and his devisiouns of ful many a subtil conclusioun.  Of this forseide scale, fro the croos-lyne un-to the verre angle, is cleped _umbra versa_, and the nether partie is cleped the _umbra recta_, or elles _umbra extensa_. And for the more declaracioun, lo here the figure.
