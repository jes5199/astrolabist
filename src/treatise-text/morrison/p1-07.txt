The border is divided into 90 degrees from the little cross (+) to the end of the meridional line under the ring. Each quadrant of the astrolabe is also divided the same way. Numbers are engraved over the degrees to divide the scale in 5 degree sections as shown by the long strokes between the numbers. Each long stroke divides the scale into a mile-way (ed. The time required to walk a mile ~ 20 minutes) and every degree represents 4 minutes of time. The figure shows the scale.
