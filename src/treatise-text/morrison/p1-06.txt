The east side of your astrolabe is called the right side and the west side is called the left side. Don’t forget this, little Lewis. Put the astrolabe ring on the thumb of your right hand and then its right side will be toward your left side and its left side will be toward your right side. Take this as a general rule that applies to the back as well as the hollow side. As I have said, there is a small cross (+) at the end of this line, which is always regarded as the first degree where the Sun rises (ed. That is, the horizon).
